Our Story

One bazaar, three stores.

AOne Bazaar started in Lahideeh, Azamgarh with a simple idea — put the supermarket, the daily kirana shop, and the neighbourhood cafe under one roof, and let people order all of it without leaving home.

🛒 AOne Bazaar

Our supermarket floor — packaged goods, household items and a wide range of everyday brands, always in stock.

🌾 AOne Kirana Store

The daily-essentials counter — atta, dal, masale, oil and the staples every kitchen restocks weekly.

☕ AOne Cafe

Coffee, tea and snacks for when you want something ready to enjoy, delivered fresh and hot.
