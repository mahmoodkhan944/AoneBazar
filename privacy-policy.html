Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AOne Bazaar ("we", "us", "our") runs a supermarket, kirana store and cafe in Lahideeh, Azamgarh, and takes orders through this website and WhatsApp. This page explains what information we collect when you use the site, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Phone number — used to sign you in and identify your orders. We don't ask for a password; your phone number is your account.
  • Name and delivery address — entered by you at checkout, used to fulfil your order.
  • Order history — what you've ordered, when, and how much you paid, so "My Orders" works and we can help if there's an issue.
  • Wishlist and reviews — products you've saved or rated, tied to your account.
  • Basic device information collected automatically by our hosting and analytics providers (e.g. browser type, approximate location from IP) — standard for any website, used only to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

Strictly to run the store: processing and delivering your orders, showing you your own order history and wishlist, replying to reviews, and contacting you on WhatsApp about an order you've placed (e.g. "your order is out for delivery"). We do not use your information for anything else.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not send marketing messages unless you've asked us to.

Where your data is stored

Order and account data is stored with Supabase, a database provider that hosts data securely on your behalf. The website itself is hosted on Vercel. Both are standard, reputable infrastructure providers used by websites worldwide — they don't access your data for their own purposes.

Payments

We do not store your UPI PIN, card numbers or banking passwords — these never pass through our systems. Online payments are made directly through your own UPI app; we only see confirmation of what you've paid via the screenshot you share with us on WhatsApp.

Cookies & local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and login between visits — this stays on your own device and isn't sent anywhere except when you place an order.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time — just message us on WhatsApp or call the number below.

Questions?

Contact us at +91 8009555567 or via WhatsApp, and we'll be glad to help.
