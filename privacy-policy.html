Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AOne Bazaar ("we", "us", "our") runs a supermarket, kirana store and cafe in Lahideeh, Azamgarh, and takes orders through this website and WhatsApp. This page explains what information we collect when you use the site, and how we use it.

What we collect

Phone number — used to sign you in and identify your orders. We don't ask for a password; your phone number is your account.
Name and delivery address — entered by you at checkout, used to fulfil your order.
Order history — what you've ordered, when, and how much you paid.
Wishlist and reviews — products you've saved or rated, tied to your account.

How we use it

Strictly to run the store: processing and delivering your orders, showing you your own order history and wishlist, replying to reviews, and contacting you on WhatsApp about an order you've placed. We do not use your information for anything else.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not send marketing messages unless you've asked us to.

Payments

We do not store your UPI PIN, card numbers or banking passwords — these never pass through our systems.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time — just message us on WhatsApp or call us.

Questions?

Contact us at +91 8009555567 or via WhatsApp, and we'll be glad to help.
